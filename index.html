Hey! I'm Luke Strong, a policy researcher and communications strategist focused on helping advocacy organizations and start-ups navigate the cannabis and psychedelic industries. I've spent over two years working within these spaces, and I'm passionately dedicated to ensuring that these substances are integrated into healthcare systems.

I'm originally from New Jersey but have bounced around the UK and US over the past few years. I completed my undergrad at Queen's University Belfast and am now back in London for a one-year MSc in Public Policy and Management at King's College London.

I'm currently working on projects for the Intercollegiate Psychedelics Network and Psilonautica, after spending over a year as a policy researcher at the Center for Psychedelic Policy. Feel free to reach out if you're interested in any of my work. I'm always happy to connect or collaborate!
